Truffle Pasta

(128 reviews)

$22.99

A luxurious plate of al dente spaghetti tossed in rich truffle oil, freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, and garnished with black truffle shavings. A true Italian classic elevated to fine dining.

Our Truffle Pasta is the crown jewel of the Savoria menu. We source our truffles directly from the Périgord region of France, ensuring the most exquisite aroma and flavour profile. The pasta is hand-rolled fresh each morning using stone-ground durum wheat and free-range eggs.

The dish is finished tableside with a generous shaving of black truffle over a silky, emulsified sauce of butter, pasta water, and Parmigiano-Reggiano aged for 36 months. It pairs beautifully with a glass of Barolo or Burgundy Chardonnay.
